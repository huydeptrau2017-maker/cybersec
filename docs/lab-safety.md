# Lab Safety and Authorization

Use the material in this repository only when at least one of these conditions is true:

- I own the target system.
- The owner has explicitly authorized the test.
- The target is an intentionally vulnerable training environment.
- The activity is within the published rules and scope of a CTF or lab platform.

Before testing, record the target, authorization, allowed techniques, prohibited actions, and end time. Never reuse lab techniques against unrelated public systems.
